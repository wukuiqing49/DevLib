# Background music selection and delivery

## Choose for the actual video

For new promotional videos, select and apply suitable background music by default. Honor an explicit silent preference, preserve meaningful source audio, and reuse suitable existing licensed music. A request for music selection authorizes routine selection, trimming and mixing within the requested deliverable; do not turn it into a mandatory approval round. It does not authorize purchases, subscriptions, account changes or replacement posts.

Match the product, audience, visual pace, reading time and intended feeling. For a calm software demonstration, a restrained instrumental electronic or ambient track is a useful starting point. Faster lifestyle footage may suit a different rhythm. Avoid hardcoding one genre, tempo, volume percentage or song for all products. Choose a phrase that starts promptly and ends cleanly; retain important sounds and keep vocals or busy melodies from competing with speech and on-screen explanations.

Use an actual track available from an authoritative source or the target account's library. Record its title, creator and ID/link. A style label or search phrase is a brief, not a selected track. Listen to the candidate in context; if audio playback is unavailable, label the choice provisional rather than claiming an audition.

## Verify the intended rights

- Confirm permission for this promotional/commercial use, edits, intended platforms, territory and any paid-ad scope actually requested. Free download or a platform search result alone is not a license.
- Prefer one track explicitly licensed across all requested platforms when producing a shared file. Otherwise use separately cleared platform versions and preserve a music-free master. Do not export music obtained through one platform's library into another platform unless its terms permit that use.
- For in-app music, check the actual account, selected track, allowed use and library restrictions. Commercial disclosure and ad authorization are separate settings; do not enable advertising permissions just to add music.
- Save the source/license URL, checked date, applicable conditions and necessary attribution in the campaign archive. Put required credits in each platform's saved description. Do not invent a license or substitute an unverified mirror when a source fails.

Official starting points, to verify against current terms and the selected track:

- [YouTube Audio Library](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/3376882): distinguish Audio Library tracks from the Shorts sound catalog and follow attribution requirements.
- [TikTok commercial music use](https://support.tiktok.com/en/business-and-creator/creator-and-business-accounts/commercial-use-of-music-on-tiktok): use music cleared for promotional content, such as suitable Commercial Music Library tracks.
- [Meta Sound Collection](https://www.facebook.com/sound/collection/): inspect the current license and destination coverage rather than assuming it applies outside Meta.

If no suitable authorized track is available, keep the music task pending, preserve the prepared video and explain the specific gap. Do not silently publish the silent fallback when music was explicitly requested.

## Mix, export and listen

Use the final output timeline for the chosen source offset, trim, output interval, looping if needed, gain, ducking and fades. For an external file, preserve the original track and record reproducible settings. For an in-app track, record the selected excerpt, controls and account preview instead of claiming that the local silent file contains music.

Balance the music by listening to the combined video, not by copying a universal volume percentage. Keep speech intelligible and leave room for important source sounds. Avoid clipping, abrupt starts/stops, distracting changes and loop seams. Check opening, transitions, CTA and ending on the actual delivery. If loudness/peak measurements are available, retain them as supporting evidence; they do not prove that the mix sounds good.

Verify duration and audio streams, decode the complete export, then listen to the rendered mix and review the text alongside it. Verify platform playback with sound enabled after publishing. An audio stream, waveform, successful encode, selected track label or muted preview does not establish audible music. Record any unheard portion explicitly.

## Existing posts and resumption

Identify the exact post and inspect whether the requested client can add/change its audio in place. Use the existing authorization for supported music edits, then read back and listen. If it cannot, preserve the post and mark audio editing unavailable for that post/client. Prepare a local music revision if useful; a duplicate or replacement upload, hiding or deletion requires its own authorization.

Track each platform independently: chosen track and rights, local versus in-app application, saved edit and audible playback. Carry the user's established music preference to all requested platforms without asking again per platform, while respecting their different capabilities and licenses.
