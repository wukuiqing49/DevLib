# Threads

Use this reference when Threads is explicitly in scope. Reuse campaign facts and authorized assets, but verify the actual Threads handle, profile, client and existing posts. An Instagram login or matching display name does not establish a Threads session or authorize creating a profile, switching accounts or enabling cross-posting. Honor the requested device; inspect only that device/account.

## Prepare a native post

Write a concise, conversational post: a real user problem, the demonstrated result and the verified product destination. Adapt the campaign copy instead of pasting a long Reel description or a block of hashtags. Use a relevant topic only when supported and appropriate; do not invent reach claims or treat plain hashtags as verified topic selections.

Check current composer limits, accepted media, upload readiness and audience/reply settings. Meta's launch documentation describes a 500-character body and videos up to five minutes; these are starting points to recheck, not permanent or API/client-equivalent guarantees. Count the complete intended body, including URLs and credits, and read it back without truncation. Do not split it into multiple published posts automatically.

Use the verified original delivery file, with readable timed text and music cleared specifically for Threads. Instagram or YouTube library rights do not automatically transfer. Inspect the actual file's track, not another platform's selected music label. If the client lacks music controls, use a cleared embedded mix; do not silently fall back to a silent or differently scored upload.

Prepare required photo/music credits and adaptation notices. Use supported author replies or an accessible credit source when the applicable license permits; preserve the required attribution rather than omitting it to fit. Record how credits will be delivered before submission. Do not substitute an inaccessible local file for a public credit link.

Inspect the uploaded preview, first frame, crop and overlay readability. A separately designed cover is an available asset, not proof the client accepts it. Use the supported cover/frame control when available; otherwise record the actual displayed frame and the limitation. Do not add the cover as an extra image, replace the media or alter the opening without a reason within the requested scope.

## Publish and verify both placements

Immediately before submission, confirm the actual handle, complete body, media revision, preview, audio choice and audience. Keep a draft request unpublished. For an authorized live trial, submit one original post, reopen it and obtain its stable share URL before adding the prepared author reply.

The early author reply names the product and repeats its real destination, adding useful context or required credits. Verify it belongs under the intended post and inspect the complete saved text. If credits need more than one reply, prepare the sequence deliberately; do not accidentally publish a second top-level post or repeat replies after a timeout. Do not call it pinned or chronologically first without evidence.

Check body and reply URL storage, clickability and final destination separately. The client may shorten link labels or change saved text: inspect the saved full text through a supported readback/edit view without resubmitting unchanged content. Correct missing or altered credit/destination URLs in the existing reply and read back again. If pasting a copied link temporarily to read it, clear both the temporary text and any automatically attached preview/quote before composing the real reply; clearing text alone may leave an attachment.

Verify published video/text display and audible playback independently of upload completion. Record the current account's post, reply, cover/frame, music, processing and external visibility states in the shared status record. An Instagram publication, a web preview, a share card or a submitted composer does not prove a native Threads video is live.

On interruption, inspect the profile's posts, existing replies and drafts before retrying. If login, onboarding, upload failure or a missing client feature blocks the trial, retain the prepared package and record the exact next step; do not create an account, change profiles, enable automatic cross-posting or publish on another platform as a workaround.
