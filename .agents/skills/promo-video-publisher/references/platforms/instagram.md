# Instagram and Reels

Confirm the actual Instagram account and Reel, Story or regular-post mode. Do not infer account identity from another platform.

Prefer a clean source master when reusing material. Check rights for any audio; a downloaded copy with another platform's watermark should not become the default when an original exists.

Inspect video preview, description, selected cover, profile-grid crop, text overlays or captions, visibility and applicable declarations. Reopen the published item and profile to confirm the cover actually displayed.

Check description, comment, profile and Story link capabilities separately. Do not infer ordinary-comment clickability from Story links. Profile editing, switching account type and paid promotion require corresponding scope. The common two-placement content package still applies; profile text does not replace a video description or author comment.

Use current official [Instagram Help Center](https://help.instagram.com/) guidance and the available client. Record account-specific restrictions and verify saved changes instead of treating an upload action as proof of success.
